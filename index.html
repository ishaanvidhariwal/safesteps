Our story

It started on a bus ride home. It is for every woman.

In 2022, two men on motorbikes chased my school bus and then followed me all the way to my house, recording me on their phones. Inside the bus, other kids laughed and told me to pose for them. I panicked and called my mother. She told me it was common, that I should be strong, not panic, and give it back to them. My father blamed me: “You must have been looking outside the window.”

SafeSteps is what I built because no girl should be told that this is normal, or that it is her fault.

Ishaanvi Dhariwal, founder, Faridabad.
