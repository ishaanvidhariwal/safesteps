Emergency Preparedness

Knowing what to do before a crisis happens can make all the difference. Here's what to have ready, who to call, and how to spot the warning signs.

Who to call

Emergency & crisis hotlines

Emergency

If you are in immediate physical danger, call:

112 (India, all emergencies)

Women Helpline (Police)

For women in danger or distress. Available 24/7.

1091

Mahila Helpline

Support and referrals for women in distress. Available 24/7. For children, call Childline on 1098.

181

Be ready

Your go-bag checklist

Keep these items somewhere quick to grab — a bag by the door, at a trusted friend's place, or in your car.

  • ID, passport, or birth certificate (copies if originals aren't available)
  • Cash and a spare phone or charger
  • House/car keys (spare set)
  • Medications and prescriptions
  • Important documents: insurance, school records, financial info
  • A change of clothes and basic toiletries
  • Numbers for trusted contacts, written down (not just saved on a phone)
Know the signs

Warning signs to take seriously

  • Extreme jealousy or controlling behavior
  • Isolation from friends, family, or support systems
  • Monitoring your phone, location, or social media without consent
  • Threats — to you, people you love, or themselves
  • A pattern of blame, guilt-tripping, or minimizing your feelings
  • Escalating anger or unpredictable outbursts
